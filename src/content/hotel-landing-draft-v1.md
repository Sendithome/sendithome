### HOW IT WORKS SECTION

**Section Title:** The Partnership Onboarding Process — Transparent, Structured, and Completed in 10 Working Days

**Step 01 — Hotel Registration (Hotel Action)**
Title: Secure Partner Registration
Description: Complete your hotel profile via our dedicated partner portal using your official hotel email address. Our programme is invitation-based and limited to verified hospitality properties. Applications reviewed within 48 business hours.
Timeline: —

**Step 02 — NDA Execution (Hotel Action)**
Title: Non-Disclosure Agreement — 5-Day Execution Window
Description: Upon profile submission, our legal team dispatches a standard mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement covering proprietary platform technology, guest data handling protocols, and partnership terms. Electronically signed via our secure document portal. No legal review fees. Simple, standard, and fair.
Timeline: 5 Calendar Days

**Step 03 — Documentation Verification (Hotel Action)**
Title: Regulatory Compliance — Trade License & Staff ID Submission
Description: Upload your valid UAE trade license and employment ID cards for designated hotel authority representatives (General Manager, AGM, Front Office Manager, and/or Head of Concierge). This one-time submission enables our compliance team to verify your property and issue your dedicated partner credentials.
Timeline: —

**Step 04 — Internal Review & Approval (SENDITHOME Action)**
Title: Our Team Reviews and Approves Your Application
Description: Our operations team reviews all submitted documentation against our hotel partner criteria. Approved properties receive a formal partnership confirmation email along with access credentials and pre-onboarding documentation. Response guaranteed within 2 business days of complete document submission.
Timeline: 48 Business Hours

**Step 05 — QR Code Issuance & Digital Activation (SENDITHOME Action)**
Title: Your Unique QR Code — Live and Active
Description: Approved partners receive a permanent, hotel-specific QR code linked to a dedicated guest-facing booking portal. Your URL is unique, immutable, and permanent: sendithomedxb.com/hotel/[your-hotel-id]. Guests access it by scanning the QR code displayed at your concierge desk, check-in counter, or in-room materials.
Timeline: Upon Approval

**Step 06 — Logistics Onboarding & Physical Installation (Logistics Action)**
Title: On-Site Logistics Setup — Completed Within 10 Working Days
Description: Our logistics partner contacts your designated property manager within 1–2 business days of approval. They arrange: (1) a staff orientation session for your concierge and front desk teams; (2) delivery of branded in-room and lobby guest communication materials; and (3) standard operating procedure briefing for the collection process. The property is fully operational within 10 working days of approval.
Timeline: 10 Working Days

**Post-Onboarding Note:**
Guest shipping is completely separate from hotel operations. Your dedicated URL is operational from Day 1. Guests access the booking portal independently — it does not interact with your PMS, OPERA, or any in-house system.
